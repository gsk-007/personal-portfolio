# Personal Portfolio PRD

## Objective

Create a portfolio that helps recruiters immediately understand:

- Who I am
- What I build
- Why I'm different
- Why they should contact me

Within 30 seconds.

---

## Target Roles

- Backend Engineer
- Full Stack Engineer
- Platform Engineer
- AI Product Engineer

Backend-heavy preference.

---

## Core Message

Full Stack Engineer with strong frontend experience, growing backend expertise, cloud experience, and a track record of shipping production systems.

---

## Site Structure

1. Hero

2. Proof of Engineering

3. Experience

4. Featured Projects

5. Other Projects

6. Tech Stack

7. About

8. Contact

---

## Hero Goals

Immediately communicate

- current role
- engineering focus
- personality
- CTA

---

## Experience Goals

Highlight achievements first.

Not responsibilities.

Each experience should answer

- What problem?
- What changed?
- What impact?

---

## Projects

Each project should include

- Overview
- Problem
- Solution
- Tech
- Key learnings
- GitHub
- Live demo (optional)

---

## Contact

Simple.

Easy to reach.

No unnecessary forms.

---

## Success Metrics

Visitors should understand

- Experience level
- Technical strengths
- Engineering mindset

Within 30 seconds.
